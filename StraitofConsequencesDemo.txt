════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
  IF JUDGES ASK COMMON QUESTIONS
════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Q: "Why is it slow?"
A: "Local Llama 3.1 8B on a laptop GPU. On a 4090 it's 15s. Latency is
   a hardware variable, not a software limit. Air-gapped is the trade."

Q: "How is the AI Red Cell different from a chatbot?"
A: "Two-layer architecture. Combat resolution is Monte Carlo with
   doctrine state machine — fast, reproducible, distribution-friendly.
   LLM only adjudicates moments where it adds value: scenario generation,
   AAR observations, response choices."

Q: "Why decision-driven instead of unit-movement?"
A: "Real wargames at RAND, CSIS, NWC Newport are decision-driven.
   Players pick ROE, force posture, doctrine. Tactical movement is
   adjudicated. Unit-movement games are a different genre."

Q: "Could the AI adjudicator generate adversarial scenarios?"
A: "Today the scenario generator + Red Cell endpoints both exist. The
   Phase 2 work is wiring them together so Iran's response after Blue's
   choice is LLM-driven, not doctrine-table-driven. We have the hooks."

Q: "What's the path to production?"
A: "AFWERX or DIU could deploy this on a SCIF-isolated network as a
   training tool for Joint Maritime Component Commanders. Doctrine
   table is hot-swappable per region. Same engine works in any theater."

Q: "How do you prevent hallucination?"
A: "Schema-validated JSON with retry-on-malformed. RAG retrieval over
   curated corpus, not freeform. Land-snap validation on AI-generated
   coordinates. We trust the LLM for prose, not for facts — every
   factual claim is grounded in a retrievable source doc."
